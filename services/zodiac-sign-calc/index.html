A quick, for-fun way to find your star sign

Western (tropical) astrology divides the year into 12 zodiac signs, each tied to a fixed range of calendar dates. This calculator takes your date of birth, matches it against those 12 date ranges, and instantly shows your sign, its element, its modality, and a light, for-fun trait blurb — the same kind of quick lookup you'd otherwise do by squinting at a printed chart. It's built purely for entertainment: a fun icebreaker, a birthday-card detail, or a quick answer to "wait, what sign are you again?"

How the 12 signs and their dates work

Each sign owns a roughly month-long slice of the calendar, based on where the Sun appears to be along the ecliptic (its path through the sky) at the time of your birth. The order and standard ranges are:

Elements and modalities, briefly

Every sign is also grouped by two extra labels that astrology tradition uses to describe its general flavor. The four elements — Fire, Earth, Air, and Water — loosely group signs by temperament (Fire: energetic; Earth: grounded; Air: idea-driven; Water: emotional). The three modalities — Cardinal, Fixed, and Mutable — describe a sign's role in each season: Cardinal signs start a season, Fixed signs hold steady through the middle of it, and Mutable signs help it transition into the next. Each of the 12 signs has a unique element-modality combination, which is why the reference table above lists both.

Worked example. Someone born on July 30 falls between July 23 and August 22, so their sign is Leo — a Fire sign with the Fixed modality. Typing "1996-07-30" (or any July 30 birthday) into the calculator above returns Leo ♌, Fire, Fixed, along with a short trait blurb.

Cusp dates: when your birthday is right on the boundary

If you were born within a day of a sign boundary — say March 20 or April 20 — you're on what's commonly called a "cusp." The date ranges shown here are the standard, widely used approximation, but the Sun's precise entry into each sign can drift by roughly a day depending on the year and your time zone, because a calendar year isn't an exact whole number of days. When your entered date lands right on one of these edges, the calculator flags the neighboring sign too, so you can see both possibilities.

Frequently asked questions

How is my zodiac sign determined from my birth date?

Your Western (tropical) zodiac sign is set by which of the 12 date ranges your birthday falls into — for example, anyone born between March 21 and April 19 is an Aries, and anyone born between October 23 and November 21 is a Scorpio. Type your date of birth into the calculator above and it instantly matches it against the standard date ranges to show your sign, element, and modality — no birth time or location needed, unlike a full astrological birth chart.

What are the 12 zodiac signs and their date ranges?

The 12 signs in order are: Aries (Mar 21–Apr 19), Taurus (Apr 20–May 20), Gemini (May 21–Jun 20), Cancer (Jun 21–Jul 22), Leo (Jul 23–Aug 22), Virgo (Aug 23–Sep 22), Libra (Sep 23–Oct 22), Scorpio (Oct 23–Nov 21), Sagittarius (Nov 22–Dec 21), Capricorn (Dec 22–Jan 19), Aquarius (Jan 20–Feb 18), and Pisces (Feb 19–Mar 20). The full table below the calculator lists every sign together with its element and modality for quick reference.

What does it mean to be born on a cusp, like March 20 or April 20?

A 'cusp' is the day or two right at the edge of two neighboring signs. The date ranges used here are the standard, widely cited approximation, but the Sun's exact entry into each sign (the solar ingress) can shift by about a day depending on the year and your time zone, since a calendar year isn't an exact whole number of days. If you enter a birth date within a day of a boundary, the calculator flags it as a possible cusp so you know both neighboring signs are worth a look.

Is this based on science, or is it just for fun?

This is a for-fun entertainment tool, not a scientific or astronomical instrument. Zodiac signs and their personality traits are part of Western astrology, a cultural and entertainment tradition — they are not supported by scientific evidence and shouldn't be used to make real decisions about health, relationships, finances, or career. Think of the trait blurb here as a lighthearted icebreaker, not a personality assessment.

Is my birth date sent to a server or saved anywhere?

No. This zodiac sign calculator runs entirely in your browser — your date of birth is only used locally to compute your sign and is never uploaded or sent to any server. The only thing saved is the last date you entered, kept in your browser's local storage, purely so it's still there if you come back to the page.
