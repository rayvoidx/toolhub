Worked examples behind the calculators

A calculator answers the question you typed. These guides cover the questions around it: which inputs change the answer most, what the formula assumes, and where the rule comes from.

By Jaehyun Park (RAYV STUDIO), who builds and maintains Toolhub · Updated 28 November 2026

Each guide follows one decision through several of the site's tools, for example the term of a mortgage, the size of an air conditioner or the cost of charging a car. The numbers in the tables come from the same calculation code the tools run, and a script kept with the site source recomputes every quoted figure and fails if one changes. Rules and thresholds are linked to the body that sets them, such as a statute, a regulator, a standards body or the original paper, and each guide says which country or standard a figure belongs to.
